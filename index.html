Junior Graphic Designer

I am Mostafa Elswaf, a passionate Graphic Designer and a student at the Faculty of Fine Arts, specializing in Photomanipulation and Branding. I enjoy transforming ideas into visually compelling designs that help brands stand out and communicate their message effectively.

My focus is on creating creative visual identities, social media campaigns, and high-quality manipulation artworks. I continuously improve my skills, explore new design trends, and strive to deliver professional work that combines creativity, strategy, and attention to detail.
